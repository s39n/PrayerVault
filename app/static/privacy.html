PrayerVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

PrayerVault is a small, self-hosted prayer journal operated by an individual, not a company. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What we store

Your prayers and prayer requests are stored as plain text files on the operator's private server. They are visible only to you when you are signed in. No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is used.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier. These are used only to create your private journal space and greet you by name. We do not share, sell, or transfer this information to anyone.

Google Drive backup

If you choose "Back up to Google Drive," the app asks Google for the drive.file permission, which only allows it to create and see files it created itself — it cannot read anything else in your Drive. The backup is a zip of your own prayers, uploaded to your own Drive, and the access token is used once and never stored.

PrayerVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features

Scripture suggestions and reflections are generated by a language model running locally on the same private server. Your prayers are never sent to any third-party AI service.

Deleting your data

You may export all of your prayers as a zip at any time from the "You" tab. To delete your account data, contact the operator at the support email shown on the Google consent screen and your folder will be removed.
